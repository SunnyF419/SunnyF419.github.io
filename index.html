About.

I approach investment research through data, economic reasoning, and reproducible analysis. I am particularly interested in how return signals behave across market conditions and what that means for portfolio construction and risk.

My work uses portfolio sorts, factor regressions, and expanding-window analysis, with attention to assumptions, robustness, and practical limitations.
